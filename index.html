BIOGRAPHY

Hailing from the heart of the capital, DJ Scott Ebbz London brings a distinctive blend of driving basslines, atmospheric soundscapes, and high-energy house rhythms to dance floors across the UK.

With years spent honing his craft behind the decks and delivering immersive sets across iconic London venues such as ExCeL London, London Olympia, The Business Design Centre, Patch London, Ruby Tuesdays, Caesars Nightclub, Weston-super-Mare Grand Pier, FLEX FM and FME Radio Hastings, Scott has built a reputation for crafting energetic journeys that resonate with those who appreciate House Music and its subgenres as well as Techno and Drum and Bass.

Scott has been a core part of Cyber House Party (CHP) since October 2020. Initially starting with graphic design, motion graphics, and the creation and distribution of merchandise, his role expanded into performing voluntary DJ sets to support the movement.

CYBER HOUSE PARTY

Scott’s involvement with Cyber House Party (CHP) since October 2020 spans both the creative and musical sides of the initiative—starting with graphic design, motion graphics, and merchandise coordination before stepping up to DJ voluntarily.

Born as a volunteer-led, not-for-profit initiative, CHP bridges the gap between the UK cyber security community, mental health awareness, and dance music. By throwing high-energy virtual and live events, the movement unites professionals and music lovers alike while channeling vital energy and donations directly into UK charities.
